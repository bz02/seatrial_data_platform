Turn how experts actually work into post-training data for AI agents: every step, the reasoning behind it, and a quality check before it counts.

Enterprise edition

Capture your own experts' real work

Runs in your network. Employees opt in, record work in approved internal apps with a browser recorder, narrate their reasoning, then review and delete steps before submitting. Data stewards approve; ML engineers export SFT and trajectory files for your own post-training.

  • Host allowlist, field masking, sensitive-term redaction, retention purge
  • Model grading off by default, or routed to your own model gateway
  • Every view, approval and export written to an audit log

Marketplace edition

Paid expert tasks in a sandbox

Credentialed experts solve realistic tasks on synthetic company data (spreadsheets and email), narrating as they go. Results are verified automatically against a hidden answer key. Approved work is packaged into datasets that we sell to model builders.

  • Programmatic verification + model-graded reasoning + human review
  • Experts earn a task reward plus a share of every dataset sale
  • Open marketplace with a take rate is built in, switched off for now

Shared core

1. Record
Event-sourced log of every action plus think-aloud narration (voice or typed).
2. Convert
Steps of reasoning → tool call → observation in seatrail.trajectory.v1; exports to chat SFT, trajectories and RL environments.
3. Assess
Verification, reasoning coverage, PII redaction, near-duplicate detection, model rubric grading, then human review.
